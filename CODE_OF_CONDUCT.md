# Community conduct

Treat contributors and researchers with respect. Discuss evidence and technical
tradeoffs; avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and
disclosure of another person's private information.

Use authorized, synthetic reproductions in public discussions. Do not publish
credentials, private targets or exploit details that belong in the private
security-reporting process described in [SECURITY](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed to protect contributors. Use GitHub's report-content and abuse-reporting
tools for conduct concerns, and keep personal information out of public reports.
This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions.
